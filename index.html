Reading these numbers
Revenue per active user grew 76.3% and key on-site engagement events grew 66% — direct evidence that the PDP, checkout and homepage changes above are converting visitors more effectively. Bounce rate also rose in the same window, which points to an entry-page (homepage) experience that isn't yet holding the growing traffic as well as it could. Beyond content and layout, this can also be a symptom of site speed — slow First Contentful Paint (FCP) and Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) cause visitors to leave before the page even finishes rendering. This design gap is exactly what the homepage redesign in the next section is built to close.
Top revenue-driving pages in this period
All Day Fibre Pro · Plant Protein (incl. new Pista variant) · Veg Collagen — the same product pages upgraded in the PDP work above.

Non-negotiable
Every design change — homepage or collection page — is shared with the brand team for review first. Development only begins after explicit approval of the design it's based on. No page goes live without a staging review and sign-off.
